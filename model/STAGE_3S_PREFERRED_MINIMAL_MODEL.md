# Stage 3S — Preferred Minimal Model Decision

Date: 2026-09-03

## Decision

**NO PREFERRED MINIMAL MODEL SELECTED.**

Canonical Stage 3S verdict:

`NO-GO — GENUINE DIRECTIONAL FRICTION ROUTE EXHAUSTED`

The candidate primitive

\[
\tau(x,y;\theta)=(x-y)^2+\theta(y-x)_+
\]

is not promoted to Stage 4 as the core of a standalone theory paper.

## Why no model is promoted

The Stage-3S search did find nontrivial algebraic effects. In particular, on the local middle-sharing price branch, added separation always increases the left firm’s candidate price but can reduce the right firm’s candidate price when directional friction is sufficiently strong. The local right-side follower problem also contains an economically interpretable trade-off between competition softening and greater directional exposure.

Those facts are **not** enough to define a publishable minimal architecture because:

1. the strongest candidate result is still a conditional price comparative static;
2. the broader asymmetric-transport literature already treats endogenous prices and locations under side-specific travel costs;
3. directional-location papers already cover first-mover effects, entry patterns, nonexistence, asymmetric dispersion, maximum differentiation and agglomeration;
4. the sharper candidate of a strategic-complement/substitute reversal in location fails its own local algebra preflight;
5. obtaining a full ordering/side-switch theorem would require solving the global price continuation across kinks and then overcoming direct Nilssen/Lai/Kharbach/van-der-Weijde prior-art threats;
6. the expected novelty gain is not commensurate with the theory complexity.

## Binding non-promotions

The following may be retained as research notes, but not as canonical Stage-4 propositions:

- local price-effect reversal for the right firm;
- local competition-softening versus directional-exposure decomposition;
- exploratory side-switching numerics;
- soft-to-hard directional limit intuition.

## What would justify reopening this primitive later

Only genuinely new external evidence or a new theorem not considered in Stage 3S should reopen the route. Examples would include:

- a proof that the one-sided soft term changes a strategic relation unavailable in all closest asymmetric/directional models;
- a general theorem applying to a class of one-sided convex frictions, not this functional form alone;
- an institutionally motivated additional strategic margin that is independently necessary and produces a new interaction rather than feature accumulation.

Absent such evidence, do not restart Stage 4 for this primitive.

## Routing

The next research action, if the broader research topic is to continue, is **Stage 3 with a genuinely distinct primitive/mechanism** or **Stage 0 with a new research question**.

Do not proceed to:

- Stage 4 Minimal Model for the current primitive;
- sequential-location full solution solely to rescue novelty;
- welfare/policy extension;
- journal positioning.
