# Prior-Art Kill Matrix

Status: Stage 3 working document. Direct paper re-opening and proposition-level mapping remain pending; this file records the mandatory comparison set and the questions that must be answered before any novelty claim is frozen.

## Novelty standard

A surviving contribution must be more than:

- a different functional form;
- a relabeling of asymmetric preferences as transport costs;
- a combination of known directional and sequential-location ingredients;
- a new numerical regime map without a new economic mechanism.

The relevant workflow test is whole-game absorption: does the full directional-friction game generate a result unavailable from any single close predecessor or immediate corollary?

## Candidate contribution statements to try to kill

### C1 — Directional price-equilibrium existence frontier

Candidate claim: one-sided soft friction changes the set of firm locations supporting a global pure-strategy price equilibrium in a directionally asymmetric way.

Kill if:

- an existing nonlinear-transport theorem maps directly to the same frontier;
- only coefficients change;
- the frontier is symmetric after relabeling or reflection and directionality adds no new economics.

### C2 — Equilibrium-constrained differentiation

Candidate claim: sequential firms choose locations partly to remain on or inside the price-equilibrium existence set, creating a location principle distinct from ordinary differentiation incentives.

Kill if:

- the follower response is identical to Nilssen or another asymmetric-location result under parameter mapping;
- the existence constraint is slack at equilibrium;
- location is still determined solely by the usual price-competition differentiation motive.

### C3 — Directional regime transition

Candidate claim: as `theta` changes, the equilibrium moves among qualitatively distinct location regimes because the directional existence frontier changes.

Kill if:

- the same maximum/interior/asymmetric/leapfrogging transition is already known in directional-location papers;
- thresholds are artifacts of interval endpoints or a chosen functional form without a general mechanism.

## Evidence discipline

No row is marked SURVIVES until the closest papers have been reopened and mapped at model/proposition level. Abstract-level similarity or absence of an exact phrase is insufficient.
