# Economics Bulletin Referee Report 2 — Core Notes

Source: referee report received with the rejection of the predecessor manuscript.

This note records only the central criticism relevant to the research restart. It is not a verbatim transcription of the full report.

## Core referee objection

The referee objected that the predecessor parameter `m` was not genuinely directional. The consumer paid the same additional term when purchasing from the designated firm regardless of whether movement was left-to-right or right-to-left.

The implication is that `m` behaved like a firm-specific disadvantage rather than a transport constraint tied to travel direction. This makes the model vulnerable to reinterpretation as vertical differentiation / firm-specific utility heterogeneity rather than direction-dependent spatial friction.

## Research consequence

The new project must not answer this criticism through wording alone. The primitive itself must vary with direction of movement.

Candidate replacement:

\[
\tau(x,y;\theta)=(x-y)^2+\theta\max\{y-x,0\}.
\]

Under this formulation, two consumers buying from the same firm can face different extra friction depending on which side of the firm they are located on.

## Workflow consequence

The criticism changes the core mechanism and therefore requires reopening theory at Stage 3. It is not classified as a bounded manuscript-level revision.
