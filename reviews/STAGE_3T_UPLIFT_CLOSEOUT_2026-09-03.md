## Hard Gate

> Does endogenous location create a theorem that exists only because E and H interact?

**Answer: NO.**

## P1 — Price-reversal frontier crossing

KILLED.

The endogenous interior stationary location is

\[
r_+(\theta)=\frac{4-3\theta+\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}}{6}.
\]

The own-price reversal frontier is

\[
r_F(\theta)=\frac{4-\theta}{2}.
\]

Their distance is

\[
r_F-r_+=\frac43-\frac16\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}\ge\frac23.
\]

Thus the endogenous stationary location is always strictly before the price-reversal frontier on the real-root branch. At the frontier, marginal location profit is already negative.

## P2 — Price-reversal-driven location regime change

KILLED as an E×H interaction result.

The endpoint-to-interior location transition occurs at

\[
\theta_L=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}\approx0.618034.
\]

But at every interior stationary point,

\[
\left.\frac{\partial p_R^*}{\partial r}\right|_{r=r_+}
=\frac{8-\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}}{9}\ge\frac49>0.
\]

Hence the location-type transition occurs entirely before the own-price-reversal region. E is not the cause of the H location-regime change.

## P3 — Private differentiation paradox

KILLED.

On the active middle branch, equilibrium demand always falls with the right firm's location:

\[
\frac{\partial D_R^*}{\partial r}<0.
\]

Thus in the price-reversal region both price and demand fall, implying

\[
\frac{\partial\pi_R^*}{\partial r}<0.
\]

The firm cannot optimally choose additional differentiation inside that region on this branch.

## Global-price validity boundary

The proven global middle-price branch supports the endogenous interior stationary point only until approximately

\[
\theta_G=\frac{126-28\sqrt2}{73}\approx1.183589.

All three proposed uplift mechanisms have already failed before this boundary. Solving alternative price/location branches beyond it would materially expand the project and violate the intended minimal uplift test.

## Scientific interpretation

The key insight is negative but useful: the price-reversal phenomenon and the endogenous-location trade-off do not reinforce each other at the firm's optimum. Endogenous location avoids the region in which further separation lowers the moving firm's price. The location problem changes type before the E reversal becomes relevant.

Therefore the uplift branch does not justify targeting a higher journal through E×H interaction.
