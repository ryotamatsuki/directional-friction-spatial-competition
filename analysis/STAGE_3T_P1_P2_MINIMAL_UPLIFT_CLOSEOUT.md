# Stage 3T — P1/P2 Minimal Uplift Closeout

Date: 2026-09-03

Base: frozen Stage 3Q EB route. This file tests only the remaining uplift hypotheses P1 and P2 after P3 was killed. It does not modify the frozen EB proposition.

## Frozen price branch

With firm L fixed at 0 and firm R at r, the middle-sharing strict global-price candidate has

\[
B(r,\theta)=4r-r^2+2\theta-\theta r,
\]

\[
p_R^*-c=\frac{B}{3},\qquad
D_R^*=\frac{B}{3(2r+\theta)},\qquad
\pi_R^*=\frac{B^2}{9(2r+\theta)}.
\]

On the active branch, `B>0`.

The own-price reversal frontier is

\[
\frac{\partial p_R^*}{\partial r}=\frac{4-2r-\theta}{3}=0
\iff
r_F(\theta)=\frac{4-\theta}{2}.
\]

The reversal region is `r>r_F(theta)`.

## Location derivative

Exact differentiation gives

\[
\frac{\partial \pi_R^*}{\partial r}
=
\frac{2(r^2+r\theta-4r-2\theta)
(3r^2+3r\theta-4r+\theta^2-2\theta)}
{9(2r+\theta)^2}.
\]

The first factor equals `-B` and is strictly negative on the active branch. Hence the sign of marginal location profit is the opposite of

\[
C(r,\theta)=3r^2+(3\theta-4)r+\theta^2-2\theta.
\]

For `0<=theta<2`, the unique positive stationary root is

\[
r_+(\theta)=
\frac{4-3\theta+\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}}{6}.
\]

On the local active branch, profit rises up to `r_+` and falls after it. Thus the constrained location optimum is `r=1` while `r_+>=1`, and then moves to `r_+` once the stationary point enters `[0,1]`.

The endpoint/interior transition solves `r_+(theta)=1` and occurs at

\[
\theta_L=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}\approx0.618034.
\]

## P1 — Price-reversal frontier crossing

P1 asks whether endogenous location `r*(theta)` crosses the price-reversal frontier `theta=4-2r`.

It does not in the minimal E×H architecture.

For the interior stationary location,

\[
r_F(\theta)-r_+(\theta)
=
\frac43-\frac16\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}.
\]

Whenever the root is real,

\[
\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}\le4,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
r_F(\theta)-r_+(\theta)\ge\frac23>0.
}
\]

Thus the endogenous interior stationary point lies strictly and substantially before the own-price-reversal frontier.

At the frontier itself,

\[
C(r_F(\theta),\theta)=\frac{\theta^2+16}{4}>0,
\]

so marginal location profit is already strictly negative there.

For the low-friction endpoint solution `r*=1`, the endpoint remains optimal only for `theta<=theta_L<2`, while own-price reversal at `r=1` would require `theta>2`. Hence the endpoint solution does not cross the frontier either.

**P1 verdict: KILL.** Endogenous location does not cross or bind the Stage-3Q price-reversal frontier on the minimal branch.

## P2 — Location-response regime change caused by price reversal

P2 asks whether the E price reversal changes the sign/curvature/optimal type of the location problem.

The natural location-type change occurs at

\[
\theta_L=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2},
\]

where the unconstrained stationary point enters the feasible interval and the optimum switches from the endpoint `r=1` to an interior `r_+(theta)`.

But this transition occurs entirely in the standard own-price-softening region. At the interior stationary location,

\[
\left.\frac{\partial p_R^*}{\partial r}\right|_{r=r_+}
=
\frac{8-\sqrt{16-3\theta^2}}{9}
\ge\frac49>0.
\]

Thus the right firm's own equilibrium price is still increasing in differentiation at every feasible interior stationary point generated by this FOC.

The location-type transition therefore precedes, rather than results from, the Stage-3Q price reversal. The E reversal does not generate the endpoint/interior switch and does not create a new FOC sign regime at the optimum.

**P2 verdict: KILL as an E×H interaction theorem.** There is a location regime change, but it is not caused by the price reversal and therefore is not a full-game-only E×H result.

## Global-price validity stop

The Stage-3P global price equilibrium condition for this middle-sharing candidate is

\[
10r^2+r\theta-4r-2\theta>0.
\]

Substituting `r_+(theta)` shows that the candidate interior optimum remains inside the proven global-price branch only up to the first positive intersection

This is well before any P1 crossing: throughout the globally validated location branch, `r*(theta)<r_F(theta)` and the own-price derivative at the interior optimum is positive.

Beyond `theta_G`, establishing a different location-price equilibrium would require reopening the global price-game classification that was deliberately excluded from this minimal uplift test. Since P1 and P2 already fail on the entire validated E×H branch, there is no justification for that expansion.
