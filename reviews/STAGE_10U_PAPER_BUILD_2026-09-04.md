## Remaining referee risks

The following are real Stage-11 attack targets but are not Stage-10 build blockers:

1. **Reduced-form absorption:** a referee may argue that the local retail game is a nonlinear market-share externality in disguise. The draft's defense is the fixed physical resource, global off-path service-floor role, and operator-envelope welfare mapping.
2. **Institutional scale:** retail demand must be material enough to influence service allocation. The draft therefore uses large destinations / clusters rather than an ordinary shop.
3. **Vehicle circulation:** independent directional service is not a literal generic two-way timetable. The approved interpretations are deadheading/short-turning, shuttles, DRT, and shared-mobility repositioning.
4. **Exogenous coverage objective:** the model does not endogenize the social value of the minimum service requirement and therefore does not derive an optimal floor.

None requires theory change before hostile review.
