## Exact blocker

**Zero-frequency / tipping multiplicity prevents certification of a global pure-strategy retail price equilibrium.**

## What passed

- the third-party fixed-fleet operator solution is exact and unique conditional on an interior shopping share;
- the local shopper-demand slope and retail price FOCs are exactly derived;
- the Stage-3 strategic-asymmetry witness satisfies demand stability and both own-price SOCs;
- the strategic-asymmetry result extends to an open local parameter set by the implicit-function theorem;
- B0/B1/B2 nested benchmarks recover correctly;
- a general reduced-form theorem identifies externality curvature as the mathematical source of opposite-signed retail price reaction slopes.

## What failed

The fulfilled-expectations shopper/operator subgame is not globally single-valued. In the symmetric M=0 benchmark with equal retail prices and 0<w/(Ft)<1/2, it has exactly three interior fixed points, only the center one locally stable.

More importantly, the exact Stage-3 local price equilibrium is not a global Nash equilibrium. With c=0,

- candidate x=11/20;
- p_L=29/70;
- p_R=261/770;
- pi_L=319/1400 about 0.227857.

Holding p_R fixed, L can cut price to about 0.327081 and move the selected stable shopping share to x=18/25. Its profit rises to about 0.235498. The deviation remains on a locally stable shopper/operator branch.

A deterministic grid audit found 920 local strategic-asymmetry candidates satisfying stability and both price SOCs; all 920 had a profitable L move toward the right saddle-node boundary of the stable shopping branch.

## Why this is not an immediate NO-GO

The failure is economically specific and admits one independently defensible repair that preserves the core mechanism: public transport normally has a minimum service obligation rather than allowing a direction to fall to zero frequency.

The core interaction remains:

`retail price -> shopping demand -> residual fleet reallocation -> waiting cost -> shopping demand`.

A minimum service floor changes only the extreme feedback that generates lock-in.

## Single authorized Stage-5 modification

Impose

\[
f_L\ge \underline f,\qquad f_R\ge \underline f,
\qquad 0<\underline f<F/2.
\]

Everything else is frozen.

Stage 5 must kill the route unless it establishes all of the following:

1. a globally well-defined shopper/operator continuation or a precise globally valid equilibrium-selection theorem;
2. a global pure-strategy retail price Nash equilibrium on a nondegenerate parameter region;
3. an open region with opposite-signed price reaction slopes at that global equilibrium;
4. exact recovery of the minimum-service-free model as the floor approaches zero where mathematically meaningful;
5. the strategic-asymmetry effect is generated by residual fleet reallocation, not mechanically by the service floor.

## Novelty warning carried forward

Even if Stage 5 succeeds, the price game reduces to a Hotelling model with a nonlinear market-share externality. Strategic asymmetry is therefore not a new concept. Stage 6 must re-kill the contribution against nonlinear network-externality Hotelling models and retain only a transport-institution theorem that follows from the fixed-fleet/frequency structure.
