## Best surviving algebraic observations

These remain research notes only.

### Local price-effect asymmetry

On the middle-sharing local candidate with right location \(r=a+d\),

\[
\frac{\partial p_L}{\partial d}>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial p_R}{\partial d}=\frac{4-2r-\theta}{3}.
\]

Larger separation can therefore lower the right firm's local candidate price for sufficiently high theta. This is a genuine algebraic result but too narrow to carry a standalone paper and remains subject to global price validity.

### Local exposure-versus-differentiation channel

A local right-side follower stationary root moves left as theta rises, consistent with a competition-softening versus directional-exposure trade-off. The channel is coherent but the outcome class is too close to known asymmetric-transport differentiation results.

### Strategic-relation reversal failed

The proposed sign switch in \(\partial BR_B/\partial a\) occurs at the same point at which the strict follower-right ordering collapses. It is not a valid within-branch strategic complement/substitute reversal.
