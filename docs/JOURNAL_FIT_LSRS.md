# Journal Fit Note — Letters in Spatial and Resource Sciences

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Fit assessment

Target: **Letters in Spatial and Resource Sciences (LSRS)**

Verdict: **GOOD FIT FOR A NARROW THEORY LETTER / NOT A BROAD GENERAL-THEORY PAPER**.

The manuscript fits LSRS because its object is explicitly spatial pricing, its main result is a clean analytical comparative static, and the paper is intentionally short. The journal's current instructions state that Letters generally should be under 10 printed pages. The current anonymous working PDF is 7 pages in the local article layout; final Springer-template pagination must be rechecked before submission.

## Why the contribution is journal-appropriate

- direct Hotelling/spatial-competition setting;
- analytical rather than calibration-driven result;
- strict global price-equilibrium audit rather than FOC-only calculation;
- exact nonempty open parameter region;
- standard quadratic Hotelling benchmark;
- clear mechanism: competition softening versus directional exposure;
- explicit scope limitation and prior-art boundary.

## Benchmark against Ago (2023)

Ago (2023), published in LSRS, is broader: it endogenizes firms' locations and obtains multiple location/price/profit results. The current paper is narrower, but its compensating strengths are a global pricing proof, an exact opposite-sign response to the same one-firm displacement, and a transparent open parameter region. The paper should not be marketed as stronger than Ago; it should be marketed as a compact distinct Letter.

## Main desk/referee risk

The largest fit risk is the objection that `r` is varied exogenously rather than chosen in a location game. The manuscript answers this by defining the research object as the pricing consequence of exogenous spatial separation. Failed endogenous-location uplift results are not imported into the paper.

2. Economics Bulletin — fallback if LSRS rejects the contribution as too narrow.
