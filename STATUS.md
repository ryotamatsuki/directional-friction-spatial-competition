\]

The primitive itself remains a legitimate exploratory representation of genuine one-sided directional friction. What is killed is the current contribution route, not necessarily every future use of this primitive.

## Killed contribution route

The following mechanism is no longer eligible as the paper's main contribution:

> directional friction → price-equilibrium existence frontier → endogenous location constrained/pinned by that frontier.

Reason: the nonlinear-transport spatial-competition literature already establishes the core mechanism linking piecewise/non-quasi-concave price profits, location-pair-specific pure-price-equilibrium regions, and restrictions on the location stage. Directional-location literature separately makes the directional/nonexistence/location overlap still stronger. Under workflow v1.1, an asymmetric version of that frontier generated by a new functional form is insufficient novelty.
