## Frozen short-note proposition

Fix the left firm at `0` and the right firm at `r in (0,1)` and let firms set uniform mill prices simultaneously.

For

\[
r\in\left(\frac{\sqrt{17}-1}{4},1\right)
\]

and

\[
4-2r<\theta<\frac{2r(5r-2)}{2-r},
\]

the Stage-3P price pair is a **strict global pure-strategy Nash equilibrium** and

\[
\frac{\partial p_L^*}{\partial r}>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial p_R^*}{\partial r}<0.
\]

Thus moving the directionally exposed right firm farther from a fixed left rival can lower the moving firm's own equilibrium mill price while raising the rival's price.

At `theta=0`, both price derivatives with respect to `r` are positive, so the opposite-sign response is generated by the one-sided directional friction.
