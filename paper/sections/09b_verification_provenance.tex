\section*{Computational verification provenance}

The symbolic and numerical verification scripts were developed with assistance from ChatGPT (OpenAI) and were subsequently reviewed by the author. Exact claims used in the propositions are enforced by symbolic identities, algebraic root counts, sign checks, and assertion-based tests in the reproducibility suite; numerical audits are labeled as supporting evidence rather than analytic proofs. The full verification gate is executed by \texttt{make verify}.
