# Cover Letter Draft — Letters in Spatial and Resource Sciences

Dear Editors-in-Chief,

Please consider the manuscript **“Directional Access Friction and the Price Effect of Spatial Separation”** for publication as a Letter in *Letters in Spatial and Resource Sciences*.

The paper studies a covered Hotelling duopoly in which consumers face a standard quadratic distance cost and an additional soft one-sided directional access cost. Holding locations fixed and solving the pricing subgame globally, the paper identifies a nonempty open parameter region in which moving the directionally exposed firm farther from its rival raises the rival’s equilibrium price while lowering the moving firm’s own equilibrium price. The mechanism is a conflict between the usual competition-softening effect of spatial separation and the increased directional exposure borne by consumers buying from the moving firm. The opposite-sign response disappears in the standard quadratic Hotelling benchmark.

The contribution is intentionally narrow. The manuscript does not claim that directional transport costs, perverse price responses to differentiation, or opposite price movements under asymmetry are new in general. Instead, it isolates a specific spatial comparative static under a strict global price Nash equilibrium and distinguishes it from the closest asymmetric-transport and Hotelling results.

The paper is designed as a concise spatial-theory Letter and is within the journal’s stated length target. It contains no empirical dataset.
